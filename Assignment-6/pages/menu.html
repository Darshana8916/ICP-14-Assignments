pizza

Pizza is a popular Italian dish originating in Naples, consisting of a flat, leavened dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and other ingredients, then baked at high temperatures. Traditional pizzas include the simple Margherita (tomato, mozzarella, basil) and Marinara (tomato, garlic, oregano, olive oil). The modern pizza is a UNESCO-recognized intangible cultural heritage, a testament to its global popularity and rich history, with the word "pizza" first appearing in a 997 AD Latin manuscript.

Burger

A burger is typically a ground meat patty, usually beef, served inside a sliced bun with condiments like ketchup, mustard, and toppings like lettuce, tomato, and cheese. They are a global staple in fast-food and casual dining, with origins tracing back to minced beef dishes in Russia and Germany before becoming popular in the U.S
